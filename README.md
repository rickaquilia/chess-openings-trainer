# Chess Openings Trainer

An interactive trainer for intermediate chess players. It teaches the strategy and ideas behind six popular openings, with a tactics trainer on the side.

## What's inside

- **Opening Explorer:** Ruy Lopez, Italian Game, Sicilian Najdorf, French Defense (Steinitz), Queen's Gambit Declined, and King's Indian Defense. Step through each line on the board with a plain-English explanation of why every move is played, plus each side's plan, the key ideas, and the traps to watch for.
- **Tactics Trainer:** six puzzles (back-rank mate, royal fork, absolute pin, Arabian mate, smothered mate, discovered attack) with hints, animated solutions, and score and streak tracking.
- **Six opening principles:** the ideas underneath all of it.

## How to use it

Open the page, pick an opening, and step through the moves. Or jump into the tactics trainer and find the best move.

No build step, no dependencies to install. Just a single HTML file.
